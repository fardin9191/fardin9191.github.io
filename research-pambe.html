Plasma-Assisted Molecular Beam Epitaxy

Overview

This work connects epitaxial growth conditions to optical behaviour in PAMBE grown III-Nitrides. It covers InGaN/GaN quantum disks and wells that show no apparent green gap, and GaN single-photon emitters whose room-temperature quantum light emission is set at the growth stage.
