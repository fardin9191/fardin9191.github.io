Machine Learning & Deep Learning

Overview

Alongside my work in photonics, I use machine learning and deep learning to solve engineering and data problems. My projects include solar irradiance (GHI) forecasting from Helioscale omega station data in Feni, which placed me among the Top Finalists at the IEEE PES Big Data Challenge Datathon 2020. I have also built CNN, DNN, and sequence models, worked on data-driven material synthesis and AI-based traffic control, and competed on Kaggle. I build my models in Python with TensorFlow, PyTorch, and scikit-learn, working in Jupyter, and I combine them with numerical optimization. My foundation comes from Andrew Ng's Machine Learning and Deep Learning Specializations.
