Top Down Fabrication

Overview

Fabricated top-down p-GaN nanowires to boost photon extraction from point-defect single-photon emitters. Also assisted on projects including Co₃O₄ modified GaN nanowire photodetectors for underwater optical communication and monolithic integration of InGaN nano-LEDs with a-IGZO thin-film transistors.
