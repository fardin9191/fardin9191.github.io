Quantum Emitter

Overview

Most quantum light sources only work at cryogenic temperatures, which keeps them stuck in the lab. My goal is quantum technology that works at room temperature, and that needs single-photon emitters that are on demand, stable, and fully reproducible. In the growth side, Plasma assisted molecular beam epitaxy gives us more growth parameters to tune and lets us control the emitter characteristics directly. Then I built an automated characterization platform (confocal microscopy, micro-PL, and HBT interferometry) and reached 99.8% single-photon purity at room temperature, a step toward practical quantum communication and sensing without cryogenics.
