About Me

Welcome to my portfolio.

I am an MSc student and research assistant at the National Institute of Scientific Research (INRS-EMT), Université du Québec, Canada, developing scalable, room-temperature, on-demand single-photon quantum emitters in Group-III Nitrides. I completed my BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET in 2024. My experimental expertise centers on Quantum Optics, nanofabrication, SPE characterization, and Metamaterials. I also have done projects on VLSI RTL-GDSII design in Cadence,robotics, automation, DFT, machine learning, electromagnetic simulation. I have multiple first-author publications, conference presentations, and 6+ awards, including the NSERC CGSM and SPIE Scholarship in 2026. I also bring leadership, teaching, mentoring, and academic outreach experience, with strong problem-solving, and a result-driven mindset.
